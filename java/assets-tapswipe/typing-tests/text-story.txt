# Real text: a short story. Narrative sentences, mixed word lengths.
The rain had stopped by the time we reached the old bridge. My brother wanted to keep
walking, but I was tired and my shoes were soaked. We sat on a bench near the river and
watched a few boats drift past. Neither of us said much. After a while he laughed and asked
if I remembered the summer we tried to build a raft. Of course I did. It sank in less than a
minute, and we were both grounded for a week!
